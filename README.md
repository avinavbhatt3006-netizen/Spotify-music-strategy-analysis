##Spotify-music-strategy-analysis

**Business Scenario**

Spotify's Music Strategy team wants to improve how it identifies, promotes, and prioritizes successful music and artists.

Spotify has limited editorial and promotional resources, so management wants to use historical song and artist data to make better decisions about where these resources should be focused.

The analysis will answer **five key** business questions:

What separates highly popular songs from the rest?
Which artists consistently perform well?
Does an artist's overall streaming performance correspond to the popularity of their individual songs?
Which artists should receive greater promotional and editorial attention?
How have music preferences and the characteristics of popular songs changed over time?

**Business Objective**

Use song-level and artist-level performance data to identify the key drivers of music success and develop data-driven recommendations for Spotify's content promotion, playlist curation, and artist strategy.

 **Dataset**

The project uses two Spotify datasets that provide complementary views of music performance:

| Dataset            |       Records | Purpose                                                                       |
| ------------------ | ------------: | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Song Dataset**   |   2,000 songs | Analyze song popularity, audio characteristics, genres, and trends over time  |
| **Artist Dataset** | 1,009 artists | Analyze artist streaming performance, catalog size, and high-performing songs |

The **Song Dataset** contains information such as artist, song, genre, year, popularity, and audio features including danceability, energy, acousticness, valence, and tempo.

The **Artist Dataset** contains artist-level metrics such as lead streams, featured streams, number of tracks, and the number of songs exceeding 100M and 1B streams.

The datasets are connected using **artist name** as the common field, allowing song-level performance to be analyzed alongside overall artist-level streaming performance.
